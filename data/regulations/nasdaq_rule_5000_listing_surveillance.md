# Nasdaq Stock Market LLC Equity Rules — Series 5000 & Market Surveillance

## Nasdaq Rule 5250 — Obligations for Companies Listed on The Nasdaq Stock Market

### Nasdaq Rule 5250(b)(1) Obligation to Make Public Disclosure of Material Information
Except in unusual circumstances, a Nasdaq-listed Company shall make **prompt disclosure** to the public through any Regulation FD compliant method (or combination of methods) of any material information that would reasonably be expected to affect the value of its securities or influence investors' decisions.

- **Pre-Notification to Nasdaq MarketWatch (`IM-5250-1`)**: The Company shall, prior to the release of the information, provide notice of such disclosure to **Nasdaq MarketWatch** at least **10 minutes prior** to public release if the release is made between **7:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time**.
- **Notification Method**: Notification to Nasdaq MarketWatch must be made electronically through the **Nasdaq Listing Center** electronic disclosure submission system (except in emergency situations where telephone notification is permitted).
- **Mandatory Material Events Requiring Prior MarketWatch Notification (`IM-5250-1`)**:
  1. Financial-related disclosures, including quarterly or yearly earnings, earnings restatements, pre-announcements, or guidance changes;
  2. Corporate reorganizations, mergers, acquisitions, or tender offers;
  3. Senior management changes of an unusual nature, or change in control;
  4. Cybersecurity breaches determined to be material under `SEC Form 8-K Item 1.05`;
  5. Bankruptcy, receivership, or defaults on senior debt securities;
  6. Regulatory investigations, FDA clinical trial decisions, or delisting notifications.

### Nasdaq Rule 4120 — Trading Halts for Material News and LULD Pauses
Nasdaq MarketWatch has the authority under `Nasdaq Rule 4120(a)(1)` to halt trading in a Nasdaq-listed security to permit the dissemination of material news (`T1` Halt — News Pending; `T2` Halt — News Released; `T3` Halt — Resumption Times Posted). While a trading halt is in effect, no Nasdaq member may execute or route orders in the halted security pursuant to `Nasdaq Rule 4120` and `17 CFR § 240.15c3-5(c)(2)(i)`.

## Nasdaq Rule 5450 & Rule 5550 — Continued Listing Standards and Minimum Bid Price Requirement

### Nasdaq Rule 5450(a)(1) & Rule 5550(a)(2) Minimum Bid Price Requirement ($1.00 Threshold)
Every Company listed on the **Nasdaq Global Select Market**, **Nasdaq Global Market** (`Nasdaq Rule 5450(a)(1)`), or **Nasdaq Capital Market** (`Nasdaq Rule 5550(a)(2)`) must maintain a **minimum bid price of at least $1.00 per share** for its primary equity security.

### Nasdaq Rule 5810(c)(3)(A) Failure to Meet Minimum Bid Price ($1.00 Deficiency & 180-Day Cure Period)
1. **Deficiency Trigger (`30 Consecutive Business Days`)**:
   A failure to meet the continued listing requirement for minimum bid price occurs when a Company's closing bid price remains **below $1.00 per share for a period of 30 consecutive business days**. Upon such failure, the Nasdaq Listing Qualifications Department shall promptly notify the Company of the deficiency (`Nasdaq Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)`).

2. **Initial Compliance Period (`180 Calendar Days`)**:
   A Company notified of a minimum bid price deficiency shall be provided a period of **180 calendar days** from the date of notification to regain compliance.
   - **Standard to Regain Compliance**: The Company regains compliance if at any time during this 180-day compliance period the closing bid price of the Company's security is **at least $1.00 per share for a minimum of 10 consecutive business days** (note: Nasdaq Staff has discretion under `Nasdaq Rule 5810(c)(3)(H)` to require up to 20 consecutive business days based on margin above $1.00, trading volume, and trend).

3. **Second 180-Day Compliance Period (`Nasdaq Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)(ii)`)**:
   - **Nasdaq Capital Market Companies** (or Global Market companies that transfer to the Capital Market prior to the expiration of the first 180-day period) may be eligible for a **second 180-calendar-day compliance period** if the Company:
     - (i) Meets the continued listing requirement for market value of publicly held shares (`MVPHS >= $1,000,000` on Capital Market) and all other initial listing standards for the Nasdaq Capital Market (`Nasdaq Rule 5505`), with the exception of the bid price requirement; AND
     - (ii) Provides written notice to Nasdaq of its intention to cure the deficiency during the second compliance period, by effecting a **reverse stock split** if necessary.

4. **Accelerated Delisting Rules (`Nasdaq Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)(iii)` & `(iv)`)**:
   - **$0.10 Low-Price Delisting Trigger (`Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)(iii)`)**: If during any compliance period a Company's security has a closing bid price of **$0.10 or less for 10 consecutive trading days**, the Listing Qualifications Department shall issue a **Staff Delisting Determination** immediately, terminating any remaining compliance period.
   - **Excessive Reverse Stock Split Restriction (`Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)(iv)`)**: If a Company fails to meet the minimum $1.00 bid price requirement and the Company has effected **one or more reverse stock splits** over the prior **two-year period** with a cumulative ratio of **250 shares or more to one** (or effected any reverse stock split within the prior 12 months under the 2025 amendment), the Company shall **not** be eligible for any 180-day compliance period and Nasdaq Staff shall issue an immediate **Staff Delisting Determination**.

## Nasdaq Rule 5605 — Corporate Governance Requirements

### Nasdaq Rule 5605(b)(1) Majority Independent Board
A majority of the board of directors of a Nasdaq-listed Company must be comprised of **Independent Directors** as defined in `Nasdaq Rule 5605(a)(2)`.

### Nasdaq Rule 5605(c)(2) Audit Committee Composition
Each Company must have, and certify that it has and will continue to have, an Audit Committee of **at least three members**, each of whom must:
1. Be an **Independent Director** as defined under `Nasdaq Rule 5605(a)(2)`;
2. Meet the criteria for independence set forth in **SEC Rule 10A-3(b)(1) (`17 CFR § 240.10A-3(b)(1)`)** under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (accepting no consulting, advisory, or compensatory fee from the issuer);
3. Not have participated in the preparation of the financial statements of the Company or any current subsidiary of the Company at any time during the past **three years**; and
4. Be able to read and understand fundamental financial statements, including a Company's balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement. Additionally, at least **one member** must have past employment experience in finance or accounting or comparable experience resulting in **financial sophistication** (`Audit Committee Financial Expert`).

## Nasdaq General 9, Section 1 & Equity 9 — Market Surveillance: Spoofing, Layering, and Wash Trading

### Prohibition on Manipulative Trading Practices (`Nasdaq General 9, Section 1(a)` & `SEA Section 9(a)(2)`)
No Nasdaq Member or person associated with a Member shall effect any transaction in, or induce the purchase or sale of, any security by means of any manipulative, deceptive, or other fraudulent device or contrivance.

1. **Spoofing and Layering (`Nasdaq SMARTS Surveillance Pattern MP-101`)**:
   - Entering non-bona-fide limit orders (often multiple price levels deep on one side of the Nasdaq order book, i.e., **Layering**) with the intent to cancel those orders before execution in order to create a false appearance of market depth or liquidity, induce other market participants' algorithms to move the NBBO, and execute a bona-fide order on the opposite side of the market.
   - Surveillance Thresholds: High **Order-to-Trade Ratio (OTR)** combined with rapid cancellation within `< 500 milliseconds` of opposite-side execution triggers immediate Nasdaq Market Regulation SMARTS alerts and cross-market CAT referral under `17 CFR § 242.613`.

2. **Pre-Arranged / Wash Trading & Self-Match Prevention (`Nasdaq Equity 4, Rule 4757(a)(4)`)**:
   - Entering buy and sell orders for the same beneficial owner that execute against each other without a bona-fide change in beneficial ownership is prohibited. Members utilizing automated trading systems are expected to enable Nasdaq **AIQ (Anti-Internalization Qualifier)** self-match prevention tags and maintain pre-trade wash-sale blocks pursuant to `17 CFR § 240.15c3-5(c)(2)(ii)`.
